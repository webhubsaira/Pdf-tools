Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 29, 2026 • Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Our Privacy First Architecture

iLovePDFs ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates on a privacy-by-design architecture. When you use any client-ready PDF tool on iLovePDFs (including Merge, Split, Rotate, Compress, Watermark, Page Numbers, Flatten, and Image conversion), your files are never uploaded to our servers.

All file parsing, page manipulation, stream restructuring, and generation are performed directly within the browser runtime using local JavaScript and WebAssembly sandbox memory.

2. File Data & Temporary Memory

Documents selected via drag-and-drop or file pickers are read into browser memory via HTML5 FileReader APIs as temporary ArrayBuffers.

  • We do not retain, copy, or index document contents.
  • Generated download links utilize temporary browser object URLs (`blob:`) which expire upon closing your browser window.
  • No document data is transmitted to analytics or advertising networks.

3. Information Collected

The Platform does not require user accounts, passwords, or credit cards to use standard tools. When you send an inquiry via our Contact form, we collect only the name, email address, and message details you voluntarily provide solely to respond to your request.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

iLovePDFs does not deploy persistent third-party tracking cookies. Minimal functional state (such as active category tabs or accessibility preferences) may be kept in your browser's local memory to enhance your direct session.
